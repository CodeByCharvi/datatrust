DataTrust Store - Warranty Policy

Laptops
All laptops come with a 2 year warranty that covers manufacturing defects. Battery wear is covered for the first 12 months only.

Phones
Phones come with a 1 year warranty. Water damage and cracked screens are not covered.

Claims
To make a warranty claim, email warranty@datatrust-store.example with your order number and photos of the problem. Claims are answered within 3 business days.
