DataTrust Store - Refund and Shipping Policy

Refunds
Customers can request a refund within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 7 business days after the returned item is received.

Electronics
Electronics such as laptops, phones and headphones have a shorter return window of 14 days. Opened software and personalised items cannot be returned.

Furniture
Furniture can be returned within 30 days, but the customer pays the return shipping cost unless the item arrived damaged. Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of delivery with photos.

Shipping
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days and costs an extra 15 dollars. Orders above 500 dollars ship free.

Support
Customer support is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM. The support email is help@datatrust-store.example.
